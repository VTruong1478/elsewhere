# Elsewhere — Product Backlog

Source: read-only product + codebase audit, 2026-08-24.

**Pass 1 (2026-08-24) — done.** Closed P0-1, P0-2, P0-3, P1-3, P1-5, P1-6, P1-7, P1-13,
plus P2-16 for the fallback case.

**Pass 2 (2026-08-24) — done.** Approved product decisions: feed leads with places (P1-2),
signup wall removed (P1-9), map zoom made request-local (P1-8), compact unrated-card state
(D1-2), and detail-page confidence from existing data (P1-4, partial — WiFi blocked on data,
see D2-1). Ranking (P1-1) and rating attributes (P1-11) were investigated only; proposals are
in the pass-2 report, no code changed.

**Pass 3 (2026-08-25) — done.** WiFi and bathroom shipped as optional rating attributes after a
design mockup round: migration `20260825120000_ratings_wifi_and_bathroom.sql` (two new enums,
nullable `ratings` columns, six `place_stats` counts, both trigger functions, backfill), rate API,
a "Features" panel in the rating form, place-detail display, and a WiFi icon on the place card.
Closes **D2-1** and **P1-4** in full. Amenity pills (**D2-3**) were deliberately not revived.
A follow-up migration `20260825140000_drop_has_wifi_and_duplicate_trigger.sql` then removed the
superseded `places.has_wifi` column and the duplicate `ratings` trigger (**D3-1**, **D3-2**).

Both passes verified end-to-end at 390px and 1440px with location granted / denied /
out-of-region, logged out and authenticated; unit, E2E, typecheck, lint and build at baseline.

Evidence comes from reading the repo, querying the running dev API, and driving the app end-to-end
(Chrome extension at ~929px; Playwright at 390px and 1440px with geolocation granted / denied /
out-of-region). Dataset at audit time: **124 active places in a 75-mile radius of the Annandale
fallback, 70 of them (56%) with zero ratings.**

Priority definitions: **P0** core journey broken, dangerous, or seriously misleading · **P1**
materially harms usability or the core value · **P2** meaningful but not urgent · **P3** polish or
lower-confidence.

## Explicitly considered and dropped

- **Duplicate venues.** Four repeated names in the feed (`Qamaria`, `Peet's`, `Weird Brothers`, `Paris Baguette`) turned out to be genuinely distinct branches at different coordinates. The real problem is that the card cannot distinguish them — filed as **P2-16**.
- **Missing photos.** Every one of the 124 live places has a usable photo ref; blank heroes in extension screenshots were a capture artefact (`naturalWidth: 800`, `complete: true`). Image *quality* and *subject* remain issues — filed as **P2-15** and noted in the report.
- **Duplicate DOM ids on the auth pages.** Asserted by a comment in `e2e/auth.setup.ts`, but `signup/page.tsx` now has three unique ids. Stale comment, not a live defect.
- **Broken pagination.** Verified correct — offsets 0/25/50 return disjoint sets. Only the cost model is wrong (**P1-12**).
